# Project-based AI & Coding Education Program

Teacher Curriculum v1.0

# Week 1. Why Coding

Theme
Why Coding

Learning Goal

- Computer follows instructions.
- Understand what an algorithm is.
- Learn that clear instructions are important.

Vocabulary

- Algorithm
- Instruction
- Step
- Order
- Computer

Activity

1. Ice Breaking
- Introduce teacher
- Introduce students
- What is coding?
1. Why Coding?
- Where do we use coding?
- Robots
- Games
- AI
- Everyday life
1. Jam Sandwich Algorithm
Students explain how to make a jam sandwich only by giving instructions.

Teacher intentionally misunderstands vague instructions.

Students debug their instructions together.

1. Reflection
What was difficult?
Why did the computer fail?

Takeaway
Computers never guess.
Good instructions make good results.
