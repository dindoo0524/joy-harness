# Session 1 — Classroom English Vocabulary & Expressions

Quick-reference expressions for teaching an English-language coding class (G3–G8 mixed). Grouped by moment in the lesson — glance and go.

## Opening

- Hi everyone, welcome to our class!
- I'm so excited to be here with you today.
- Today we're going on a coding adventure together.
- Are you ready for a new challenge?
- Let's discover something new today.
- This is going to be a fun journey — let's get started!

## Appreciation

- Thank you so much for being here today.
- I really appreciate your energy today.
- This is such a great opportunity for us to learn together.
- I'm excited we get to spend this time together.
- Thank you for trying so hard, everyone.

## AI & Coding

- A robot follows commands, just like this.
- This is called an instruction — a robot needs instructions to move.
- We're going to write a program today.
- A program is just a set of instructions for a machine.
- Can you give the robot a command?
- What's the task we need to solve?
- What's the problem here, and what's our solution?

## Why Coding?

- Coding helps us solve problems.
- Coding can automate things — that means the machine does it for us.
- It makes our life easier and faster.
- Coding helps us repeat the same task easily, again and again.
- We use technology like this every single day.
- Coding gives you the power to create things.

## Where?

- Where do we see coding in everyday life?
- Think about a traffic light — it follows a program too.
- A washing machine follows a program.
- What about a microwave? It follows instructions too.
- Your smartphone runs on code.
- A vending machine follows commands.
- Drones are controlled by code.
- Even this game was made with code.
- An elevator follows a program to stop at each floor.

## Robot

- The robot is your teammate today.
- Think of the robot as your helper.
- You and the robot are partners.
- The robot is an explorer — where should it go?
- Who's going to be the captain and give the commands?
- The robot is your assistant — tell it exactly what to do.
- What's our mission today?

## Jam Sandwich

- What ingredients do we need?
- We need strawberry jam.
- Where's the knife?
- Use the spoon to get the jam.
- Now spread the jam on the bread.
- Cut the bread into a slice.
- Put the bread on the plate.

## Teacher Commands

- Listen carefully.
- Think about it first.
- Let's try it.
- Take a guess.
- Discuss it with your team.
- Share your idea with the class.
- Show me what you've got.
- Can you explain that to me?
- Choose one answer.
- Write it down.

## Encouragement

- Great job!
- Nice idea!
- Good try!
- Almost there!
- Let's try again.
- Don't worry about it.
- Keep going, you're doing great.
- One more time — you can do it.
- Excellent work!

## Team Activity

*For the robot-instruction activity: two teams (Nant team / Immanuel team) take turns giving the teacher, playing the robot, instructions — then revise after a failed attempt.*

- Nant team, come together and huddle up.
- Immanuel team, it's your turn now.
- Take turns giving me instructions — one at a time.
- One instruction at a time, please.
- Okay, let's see what happens.
- That didn't work — let's figure out why.
- Which team's robot moved closer to the goal?
- Let's compare our results.
- It's okay — let's fix the instruction and try again.
- Great teamwork, Nant team!
- You got it this time — well done, Immanuel team!
- Let's give both teams a big round of applause!
