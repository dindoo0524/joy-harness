Mission Card 02

Recycling Helper

Your Mission

Create a service that helps students recycle correctly at school.

The Problem

Students sometimes don't know where to throw away their trash.

For example:

  • Plastic
  • Paper
  • Glass
  • Cans

Your Goal

Design a simple helper that teaches students the correct recycling bin.

Your Team Can Create

  • A paper screen
  • Item cards
  • Recycling bin cards
  • Picture buttons
  • A simple flowchart
  • A role-play demonstration

Think About

  • Is it easy to use?
  • Is it easy to understand?
  • Can younger students use it?
  • Does it make recycling easier?

Remember

There is no single correct answer.

Work together. Test your idea. Improve it. Have fun!
